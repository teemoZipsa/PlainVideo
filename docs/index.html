Local video player for Windows

Open a video. Keep the screen for the video.

PlainVideo is a borderless player for local files on Windows 10 and 11 (x64). Playback controls appear when you need them and get out of the way when you watch.

PlainVideo는 Windows 10·11(x64)용 로컬 동영상 플레이어입니다. 파일을 열면 영상에 집중할 수 있고, 재생 조작이 필요할 때만 컨트롤이 나타납니다.

Get it from Microsoft Store Portable downloads

Local playback

Open a file with drag and drop or Ctrl + O. Choose subtitles and audio tracks, resume a partly watched file, or save a screenshot.

No account or ads

Your media and viewing history stay on your device. PlainVideo has no account, advertising, analytics, or automatic telemetry.

Need a hand?

The help page lists the main controls and where to report a problem. The privacy policy explains what the app stores locally. Both pages include Korean and English.
